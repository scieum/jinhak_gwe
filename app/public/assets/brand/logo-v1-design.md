## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished production-ready horizontal logo asset for a Korean university admissions planning web app named exactly "진학내비". Transparent background with genuine alpha. Wide horizontal composition, approximately 4:1 aspect ratio; logo should occupy nearly the full canvas with modest 5% safe padding. On the left a distinctive compact navigation symbol: a bold cobalt-blue (#0066FF) route that turns smoothly and resolves into a crisp northeast-pointing navigation arrow, with a small starting waypoint integrated into the route. The route and arrow should read as ONE balanced geometric mark, suggesting guiding a student's next step and finding a direction. No map background, no compass letters, no graduation cap, no school building, no shield. To the right, exact Korean wordmark "진학내비", four Hangul syllable blocks, perfectly legible, dark charcoal #141414, confident modern Korean sans serif lettering, medium-heavy weight, balanced spacing, aligned optically with the icon. Restrained flat two-color vector-like graphic, immaculate silhouette, no texture, no shadow, no gradient, no mockup, no labels, no slogan, no surrounding card or border. This is the final logo artwork itself, not a presentation sheet. It must remain legible at 180 pixels wide on white and light gray app navigation surfaces. Deliver only this one logo, centered on transparent background.
